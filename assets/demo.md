# Notes on the first draft

The report was written in a hurry, and it shows. There are a number of
places where the argument is really quite weak. The reader has to work
very hard to follow it.

When the first section was drafted, the team decided to include every
single finding from the survey, along with the methods, the caveats and
the open questions, so that nobody could later say the results had been
hidden or softened in any way.

The second section tries to utilize the same approach, but it spends
most of its length explaining terms that its readers already know before
it finally gets to the point.

Most of these problems are easy to fix. Cut the setup. Say the thing.
Short sentences help. Active verbs help more.
